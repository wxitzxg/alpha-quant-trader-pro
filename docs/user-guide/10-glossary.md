## A-C

### Account (账户)
A record that tracks a user's cash balance, positions, and transaction history.

### Average Cost (平均成本)
The weighted average price paid for all shares of a stock in your position.

**Formula**: `Total Cost / Total Quantity`

---

### Backtest (回测)
Testing a trading strategy on historical data to evaluate its performance.

### Backtest Engine (回测引擎)
The system component that executes backtests and calculates performance metrics.

### Bear Market (熊市)
A market condition where prices are falling or expected to fall.

### Bid Price (买价)
The highest price a buyer is willing to pay for a stock.

### Bollinger Bands (布林带)
A volatility indicator consisting of a moving average and two standard deviation bands.

---

### Candlestick (K线/蜡烛图)
A chart that displays open, high, low, and close prices for a period.

### Commission (佣金)
Fee charged by broker for executing trades (typically 0.03%).

### Confidence Level (置信度)
A rating (1-5) indicating the reliability of an analysis signal.

### Cup and Handle (杯柄形态)
A bullish continuation pattern resembling a cup with a handle.

---

## D-F

### Death Cross (死亡交叉)
When short-term moving average crosses below long-term MA (bearish signal).

### Divergence (背离)
When price and indicator move in opposite directions, signaling potential reversal.

### Drawdown (回撤)
The decline from a peak to a trough in portfolio value.

**Formula**: `(Peak Value - Trough Value) / Peak Value × 100%`

---

### Entry Price (入场价)
The price at which you buy a stock.

### Exit Price (出场价)
The price at which you sell a stock.

### Exponential Moving Average (EMA, 指数移动平均)
A moving average that gives more weight to recent prices.

---

### Fee (费用)
Total cost of a trade, including commission and taxes.

### Fibonacci Retracement (斐波那契回撤)
Horizontal lines indicating potential support/resistance levels.

### Five-Dimensional Resonance (五维共振)
Scoring system evaluating stocks across 5 dimensions (trend, pattern, position, momentum, trigger).

---

## G-I

### Golden Cross (黄金交叉)
When short-term moving average crosses above long-term MA (bullish signal).

### Holding Period (持仓周期)
The duration for which you hold a position.

### High (最高价)
The highest price during a trading period.

---

### Indicator (指标)
A mathematical calculation based on price/volume data used for analysis.

### Long Position (多头仓位)
Owning shares of a stock (betting price will rise).

### Low (最低价)
The lowest price during a trading period.

---

### Market Order (市价单)
An order to buy/sell at the current market price.

### Market Value (市值)
Current value of your positions.

**Formula**: `Quantity × Current Price`

### Moving Average (MA, 移动平均)
Average price over a specified period.

---

## J-L

### K-Line (K线)
Chinese term for candlestick chart.

### Limit Order (限价单)
An order to buy/sell at a specified price or better.

### Liquidity (流动性)
How easily a stock can be bought/sold without affecting its price.

---

### Long-term MA (长期均线)
Moving average with longer period (e.g., 60-day, 120-day).

### Loss (亏损)
Negative profit from a trade.

---

## M-O

### Market Capitalization (市值)
Total value of a company's outstanding shares.

**Formula**: `Share Price × Total Shares`

### Momentum (动量)
The rate of acceleration of a stock's price movement.

### Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD, 异同移动平均线)
Trend-following momentum indicator.

---

### OBV (On-Balance Volume, 能量潮)
Volume-based indicator that uses cumulative volume flow.

### On-Balance Volume (能量潮)
See OBV.

### Open (开盘价)
The first price at which a stock trades during a period.

---

### Overbought (超买)
When a stock's price has risen too high and may reverse down.

### Oversold (超卖)
When a stock's price has fallen too low and may reverse up.

---

## P-R

### Pattern (形态)
A recognizable configuration of price movements on a chart.

### P&L (Profit and Loss, 盈亏)
The net gain or loss from trading.

### Position (仓位)
The amount of a security you own.

---

### Position Size (仓位大小)
The number of shares or percentage of portfolio allocated to a trade.

### Portfolio (投资组合)
A collection of investments held by an individual or institution.

### Realized P&L (已实现盈亏)
Profit/loss from completed trades (sold positions).

---

### Relative Strength Index (RSI, 相对强弱指标)
Momentum oscillator measuring speed and change of price movements (0-100).

### Resistance (阻力位)
Price level where selling pressure is expected to be strong.

### Return (收益率)
Profit or loss expressed as a percentage.

**Formula**: `(Current Value - Initial Value) / Initial Value × 100%`

---

### Risk-Reward Ratio (风险回报比)
Ratio of potential profit to potential loss.

**Formula**: `(Target Price - Entry Price) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss)`

### RSI (相对强弱指标)
See Relative Strength Index.

---

## S-U

### Sharpe Ratio (夏普比率)
Measure of risk-adjusted return.

**Formula**: `(Return - Risk-Free Rate) / Standard Deviation`

### Short Position (空头仓位)
Selling borrowed shares (betting price will fall). **Not supported** in this system.

### Short-term MA (短期均线)
Moving average with shorter period (e.g., 5-day, 10-day).

---

### Signal (信号)
An indication to buy or sell based on technical analysis.

### Simple Moving Average (SMA, 简单移动平均)
Equal-weighted moving average.

### Slippage (滑点)
The difference between expected price and actual execution price.

---

### Stamp Duty (印花税)
Tax on stock sales (0.1% in China).

### Stop-Loss (止损)
Pre-determined price level to sell and limit losses.

### Stochastic Oscillator (随机指标)
Momentum indicator comparing closing price to price range.

---

### Support (支撑位)
Price level where buying pressure is expected to be strong.

### Swing High/Low (波段高点/低点)
Short-term peaks and troughs in price movement.

---

## V-Z

### Volume (成交量)
The number of shares traded during a period.

### VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern, 波动收缩形态)
A pattern where volatility decreases before a breakout.

### Volatility (波动率)
A statistical measure of price dispersion.

---

### Win Rate (胜率)
Percentage of winning trades.

**Formula**: `Winning Trades / Total Trades × 100%`

### Win/Loss Ratio (盈亏比)
Ratio of average win to average loss.

---

## 📊 Technical Analysis Terms

### Bullish (看涨)
Expecting price to rise.

### Bearish (看跌)
Expecting price to fall.

### Breakout (突破)
When price moves above resistance or below support.

### Breakdown (跌破)
When price falls below support.

---

### Consolidation (整理)
Period of sideways price movement.

### Continuation Pattern (持续形态)
Pattern suggesting trend will continue.

### Reversal Pattern (反转形态)
Pattern suggesting trend will reverse.

---

## 📈 Chart Patterns

### Head and Shoulders (头肩形态)
Reversal pattern with three peaks.

### Double Top/Bottom (双顶/双底)
Reversal pattern with two peaks/troughs.

### Triangle (三角形)
Continuation pattern with converging trendlines.

### Flag/Pennant (旗形/三角旗形)
Short-term continuation patterns.

---

## 🎯 Strategy Terms

### Trend Following (趋势跟踪)
Strategy that buys in uptrends and sells in downtrends.

### Mean Reversion (均值回归)
Strategy that bets prices will return to average.

### Breakout Trading (突破交易)
Strategy that buys when price breaks resistance.

### Momentum Trading (动量交易)
Strategy that buys stocks with strong upward momentum.

---

## 💡 Risk Management Terms

### Position Sizing (仓位管理)
Determining how much to invest in each trade.

### Diversification (分散投资)
Spreading investments across different assets.

### Risk Per Trade (每笔交易风险)
The maximum amount you're willing to lose on a single trade.

---

## 🔢 Performance Metrics

### Annual Return (年化收益率)
Return expressed as yearly rate.

### Maximum Drawdown (最大回撤)
Largest peak-to-trough decline.

### Standard Deviation (标准差)
Measure of volatility/ risk.

### Alpha (阿尔法)
Excess return relative to benchmark.

### Beta (贝塔)
Volatility relative to market.
